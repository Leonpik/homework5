Experiment description: before including reset.css, the h1 heading has a large bold font and its own margin, the list shows bullet markers and left indent, the quote is wrapped in quotation marks, and the link is underlined and blue. After including reset.css, all these elements are "stripped bare": the heading becomes plain text with no bold weight or margin, list markers disappear, the quotation marks are removed, and the link loses its underline and color. This is a much harsher reset than normalize.css: reset.css does not align browser styles, it removes them almost entirely, so afterwards you have to rebuild the appearance of every element from scratch.
